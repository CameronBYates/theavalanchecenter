Alps avalanche bulletins

Find the official avalanche warning services for the Alps in France, Switzerland, Austria, Italy, Germany and Slovenia.

In the Alps, avalanche bulletins come from national and regional warning services, so the right one depends on the country and region you are visiting. Some bulletins are only published in the local language, and those are marked in the list. The European Avalanche Warning Services map brings the European bulletins together in one place.
